Before You Go

Make the Most of Your Night

The Northern Lights are a natural phenomenon, so sightings depend on solar activity, darkness, cloud cover and weather. A sighting cannot be guaranteed.

Important information
This tour is weather-dependent. If conditions are unsuitable, your driver may need to adjust the route or discuss alternative arrangements with you. Please dress warmly and bring suitable winter clothing.
